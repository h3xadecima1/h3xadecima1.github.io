Project overview

The team firmware combines three TCS3200 color sensors, a TCS34725 color sensor in the line-following program, ultrasonic ranging, four PWM motor channels and servo-driven gripper and arm control.

Engineering implementation

  • Implemented state-machine logic for navigation and object pickup/placement in the team codebase.
  • Used per-sensor color calibration and distance-based decisions.
  • Included mechanical CAD drawings, vehicle calibration programs and motor/sensor test code.

Results & scope

The repository documents vehicle hardware and separate line-following and obstacle-avoidance implementations. Quantitative course performance and individual team contributions are not established by the inspected files.
