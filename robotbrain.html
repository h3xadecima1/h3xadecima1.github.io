Project overview

The final KiCad 9 board uses an STM32F103RCTx microcontroller and two copper layers. The design integrates USB, SWD, UART, an MPU6050 interface, a Bluetooth module, ultrasonic and drive connectors, encoder signals and a power-monitoring ADC input.

Engineering implementation

  • Designed a two-layer PCB with 386 routed copper segments in the final design file.
  • Integrated USB protection, clock circuitry, reset and boot controls, and RGB indicator interfaces.
  • Prepared schematic, bill of materials and Gerber fabrication outputs.

Results & scope

The repository contains the completed layout revision and manufacturing files. Physical assembly and functional bench-test results have not been independently established.
